Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

Brad's Health Sync is a personal application used only by its owner, Brad Payne. It is not offered to the public.

Data accessed

The app uses Google OAuth to read the owner's own Google Health data (such as steps, sleep, heart rate, weight and nutrition logs). It accesses no one else's data.

Use and storage

The data is used only by the owner for personal health tracking and analysis. It is stored privately in the owner's own accounts and devices.

Sharing

The data is not shared with, sold to, or disclosed to any third party, and is not used for advertising.

Deletion

The owner can revoke the app's access at any time from their Google Account security settings and delete the stored data.
